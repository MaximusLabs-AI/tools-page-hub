Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Peec AI replace my SEO tool?

No. AI-visibility tracking and traditional rank tracking measure different surfaces. Peec AI measures whether AI engines mention your brand; your SEO suite measures Google SERP position. They are complementary.

Which AI engines does Peec AI track?

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini are the confirmed core, with additional engine coverage referenced by the vendor. For the exact current list, check peec.ai, as coverage changes.

Peec AI vs Profound, which should I pick?

Peec AI is the best-value mid-market pick; Profound is the enterprise, analyst-grade option with the broadest coverage and custom pricing. If you need depth and have budget, look at Profound; otherwise Peec AI wins on value.

Is there a free version?

Peec AI offers a trial rather than a permanent free tier. If a free tier is a hard requirement, AthenaHQ is the closest alternative that starts free.
