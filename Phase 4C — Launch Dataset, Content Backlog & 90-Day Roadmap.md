## 12. Programmatic SEO Publication Policy

- No page is auto-generated from a template without a human-reviewed minimum-original-content check (Phase 4B QA rule).

- Filtered category views only graduate to indexable pages after reaching the 10+ unique result threshold set in Phase 3.
- All programmatic candidate pages enter a review queue; Editorial Lead approves before indexing, capping unchecked-page proliferation.

## 13. Search, Filtering & Sorting Specification

- **Search**: full-text across tool name, description, category; typo-tolerant, autocomplete-enabled.
- **Filters**: pricing model, company size, deployment, privacy positioning, open-source status, agency support, enterprise readiness — all pulling from Phase 4A's controlled vocabulary, never free text.
- **Sorting**: default = fit-label rank (Best fit first) within the active category context, never a raw popularity metric; secondary sort options = price (low-high), last-verified date.
- Filter combinations producing zero or near-zero results display "No tools currently match — broaden your filters" rather than a broken empty state.

## 15. Commercial Model with Trust Protections

- Sponsored placements are visually labeled "Sponsored," physically separated from organic rankings, and structurally blocked from affecting `rankings.numeric_score` via the hard database constraint established in Phase 4A/4B.
- Affiliate links carry a visible above-the-fold disclosure banner on any page containing them.
- No vendor may purchase a "Best fit" label — vendors may only sponsor placement position within an already-computed organic ranking, never alter the ranking itself.
- Vendor self-corrections require independent corroboration before acceptance (Phase 4B rule), preventing paid placements from also inflating feature claims.
